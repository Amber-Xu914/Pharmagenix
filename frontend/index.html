    About Pharmagenix

    Pharmagenix helps researchers and students explore how drugs interact with a gene: which drugs inhibit, induce or bind the gene's product, which drugs it metabolises, and how its genetic variants change a patient's response. Search one gene to list every drug that interacts with it, or upload a tumour's somatic variants (VCF / MAF) with its cancer type to get ranked candidate drugs for its actionable variants (lung cancer prototype, synthetic test data only).

    Each search combines curated reference datasets (HGNC, ChEMBL, CPIC and the Australian PBS Schedule) with live evidence from DGIdb, PharmGKB/ClinPGx, PubTator3, UniProt and DrugBank. The evidence for each gene–drug pair is merged and graded by fixed rules, then a large language model writes a summary in which every claim cites its evidence. When nothing is found, no summary is generated. The table and network are built directly from the source data, never by the model.
